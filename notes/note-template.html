1. Setting

Write in ordinary prose with inline math such as $u : \Omega \times (0,T) \to \R$, where $\Omega \subset \R^n$ is a bounded domain. Displayed, numbered equations look like this:

\begin{equation}\label{eq:sample} \partial_t u - \Delta u = f \quad \text{in } \Omega \times (0,T). \end{equation}

and you can refer back to \eqref{eq:sample} anywhere.

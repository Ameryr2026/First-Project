About

I am a final-year PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at Wayne State University, expecting to complete my degree in May 2027. My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, with a focus on the mathematical theory of liquid crystal flows.

Before graduate school, I taught secondary-school mathematics in Jordan. Teaching remains a central part of my work. On this site I write open notes in two languages: in English, on how research in nonlinear PDEs is actually done; in Arabic, the foundations of mathematics with Arabic notation, for readers who have few sources in their own language.

English projects

Notes on how research in nonlinear PDEs works, and interactive pages for teaching.

  • The Analysis of Nonlinear PDEs How existence and uniqueness are proved, through a real case study and the tools it uses, linked as a wiki, with exercises and solutions (draft). Also as a printable PDF.
  • Riemann Sums An interactive introduction: rectangles, upper and lower sums, and the definite integral.
  • Riemann Sums with JSXGraph The same idea written directly in code, as a starting point for your own interactive graphs.
  • Source code and guides Everything on this site is open: how to edit it, reuse it, or build something similar.

Research

My dissertation studies Ericksen's model for nematic liquid crystals with variable degree of orientation, working with a reduction of the system to Poiseuille flow.

Advisors: Fernando Charro and Tao Huang
